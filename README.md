# Hi, I'm songpoo

**Customer Transformation Strategist**

I turn complex customer and operational problems into clear requirements, practical solution designs and coordinated action.

My background combines external client consulting at **IBM** with internal transformation at **Samsung Electronics DS**. This gives me both perspectives: understanding a customer's business problem and navigating the constraints that shape technology adoption inside an enterprise.

## What I Bring

- **Customer discovery:** Using research and workshops to uncover workflow problems, clarify requirements and align stakeholders around the problem worth solving.
- **AI use-case qualification:** Assessing where AI can help, what data and organizational conditions are needed, and what should be validated before implementation.
- **Solution shaping:** Connecting business needs with workflow design, technical options and concrete requirements for engineering collaboration.
- **Adoption and enablement:** Designing and running programs that help people apply AI to their work, with clear responsibilities and feedback loops.
- **Cross-functional leadership:** Leading process redesign and coordinating business, design and technical teams without losing sight of user needs.

UX and research are the foundation of my approach. I look at the people, workflows and systems together rather than starting with a technology and searching for a use case.

## Selected Public Project

### [Multi Tokens — Structured Design Handoff](https://github.com/songpoo/multi-tokens)

A JSON-based design-token reference that makes reusable design decisions explicit and inspectable.

**What it contains**
- Core, light, dark and theme token sets
- Structured definitions for reusable visual properties
- Documentation for design-to-engineering handoff

**How it connects to my work**

Clear requirements and shared definitions matter when different disciplines need to build together. This project shows one way to translate design decisions into structured data that designers and engineers can discuss and reuse.

It demonstrates structured specification and collaboration, not an AI agent or production deployment.
